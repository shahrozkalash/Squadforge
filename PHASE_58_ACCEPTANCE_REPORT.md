## Known limitations

**TIFF decode** refuses by name — never silently: Deflate (compressions 8 and 32946), JPEG-in-TIFF (6/7), CCITT (3/4), tiled layout, BigTIFF, CMYK/YCbCr/CIELab photometrics, float (SampleFormat 3) or signed samples, predictor 3, mixed bit depths, and bit depths other than 8/16. The orientation tag is reported in metadata but not applied to pixels. Multi-page files decode page 1 only (the page count is reported). TIFF **encode** is uncompressed-only. PackBits is decoded as one continuous stream per strip; the row-aligned packetization real writers use reads identically in the engine and ffmpeg (a row-spanning packet is where ffmpeg's per-row accounting and the stream reading diverge — observed and documented, not hidden). **JPEG** has no alpha channel (a property of the format; RGBA input encodes from RGB), CMYK JPEGs are not fixture-verified and are not claimed, and EXIF/ICC are detected and reported as present but not parsed or applied. PNG **iTXt** chunks are not decoded (tEXt and zTXt are). **PSD** keeps exactly its Phase 49 support and warnings. In the **desktop app**, PSD and TIFF imports are refused by name (the TS engine is the path for those formats), JPEG quality is a pinned constant 90 rather than a user slider, and — the standing caveat — **the generated GUI remains compile-verified only and has never been run** (no display on this VM); its file-handling *core* is what the harness executed (S14), not the dialogs themselves.
